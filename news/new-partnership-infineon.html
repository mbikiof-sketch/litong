BeiLuo Expands Product Line with New Partnerships

By BeiLuo Team • February 20, 2026 • Source: BeiLuo Press
← Back to News

BeiLuo is proud to announce strategic partnership agreements with multiple leading semiconductor manufacturers, significantly expanding our product portfolio and strengthening our position as a premier electronic components distributor.

Expanded Manufacturer Partnerships

Through these new partnerships, BeiLuo now offers authorized distribution for an expanded range of products including advanced power modules, next-generation microcontrollers, and specialized sensor solutions. These agreements enable our customers to access the latest semiconductor technologies with guaranteed authenticity and full manufacturer warranty support.

Enhanced Product Portfolio

The expanded portfolio includes new IGBT and SiC power modules for EV and renewable energy applications, advanced MCU families for industrial automation, and high-precision sensors for automotive and IoT applications. With these additions, BeiLuo now offers over 50,000 products across more than 100 leading brands.

Improved Technical Support

As part of these partnerships, BeiLuo is investing in expanded technical support capabilities. Our FAE (Field Application Engineering) team has been strengthened with specialists trained by our new manufacturing partners, enabling us to provide deeper technical consultation and design-in support for our customers.

Commitment to Quality

All products distributed through these new partnerships are sourced directly from the manufacturers with full traceability documentation, ensuring 100% genuine quality. BeiLuo maintains strict quality control processes and ISO 9001 certified operations to guarantee customer satisfaction.
