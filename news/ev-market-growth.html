EV Market Drives Power Semiconductor Demand

By BeiLuo Analytics • March 10, 2026 • Source: Market Research
← Back to News

The accelerating global transition to electric vehicles is creating an unprecedented surge in demand for power semiconductors, particularly IGBT modules, SiC MOSFETs, and GaN power devices.

EV Market Growth Trajectory

Global EV sales are projected to exceed 30 million units in 2026, representing over 35% of total new car sales. This rapid adoption is driving massive demand for power semiconductors throughout the EV powertrain, including traction inverters, onboard chargers, DC-DC converters, and battery management systems.

SiC and GaN Revolution

Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) power devices are at the forefront of the EV semiconductor revolution. SiC MOSFETs offer superior efficiency, higher voltage ratings, and better thermal performance compared to traditional silicon IGBTs, making them ideal for EV traction inverters. Major manufacturers including Infineon, STMicroelectronics, and onsemi are investing heavily in SiC production capacity.

GaN devices are gaining traction in onboard chargers and DC-DC converters, offering higher switching frequencies that enable smaller, lighter, and more efficient power systems. The GaN power device market is expected to grow at a CAGR of over 40% through 2030.

IGBT Remains Relevant

Despite the rise of wide-bandgap semiconductors, IGBT modules continue to play a crucial role in EV powertrains, particularly in mainstream and commercial vehicle applications. Infineon's EDT2 and Semikron's SKiM technologies continue to evolve, offering improved power density and reliability at competitive costs.

Supply Chain Considerations

Distributors with strong relationships with manufacturers, robust inventory, and technical expertise can help OEMs secure supply and optimize their component sourcing strategies.
