## 2.3 Competitor Analysis (竞品分析)

### 3peak Market Position

**Strengths**:
- Leading domestic high-performance analog IC design company in China
- Full-category signal chain chip capabilities (rare among domestic companies)
- Cost advantage: 30-50% lower than international brands
- Better supply availability and shorter lead times
- Local technical support in Chinese
- IATF 16949 automotive certification
- AEC-Q100 qualified products available

**Key Differentiators**:
- Pin-compatible alternatives to TI, ADI, Maxim
- Import substitution leader in China
- Strong in precision op-amps (50μV offset)
- 16-bit ADC (TPC1610) competing with AD7980/ADS8319
- Comprehensive signal chain portfolio

### Primary Competitors

#### 1. Texas Instruments (TI)
- **Strengths**: Broadest portfolio, strong brand recognition, extensive resources
- **Weaknesses**: Higher prices, longer lead times, less flexible for China market
- **Overlap**: Operational amplifiers, ADCs, power management

#### 2. Analog Devices (ADI)
- **Strengths**: Precision analog leader, strong in data converters
- **Weaknesses**: Premium pricing, limited local support in China
- **Overlap**: Precision op-amps, ADCs, voltage references

#### 3. Maxim Integrated (now ADI)
- **Strengths**: Interface products, power management
- **Weaknesses**: Being integrated into ADI, supply uncertainty
- **Overlap**: Interface chips, power management

#### 4. Other Domestic Chinese Brands
- **SGMICRO**: Similar product range, strong competitor
- **SILERGY**: Focus on power management
- **Navitas**: Power semiconductors

### Our Competitive Advantages as Distributor
1. **Authorized status** - Direct 3peak partnership
2. **Local FAE support** - Technical assistance in Chinese
3. **Import substitution expertise** - Help customers transition from TI/ADI
4. **Fast delivery** - Local inventory in China
5. **Cost advantages** - 30-50% savings vs international brands
6. **Reference designs** - Complete solutions available
