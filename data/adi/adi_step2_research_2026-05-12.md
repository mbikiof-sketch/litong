## 2.3 Competitor Analysis (竞品分析)

### ADI Market Position

**Strengths**:
- Industry leader in data converters
- Broadest analog portfolio (after Maxim + Linear acquisition)
- 32-bit ADC technology leader
- Strong RF/microwave offerings
- AEC-Q100 automotive qualified products

**Key Differentiators**:
- Highest resolution ADCs (32-bit)
- Comprehensive signal chain solutions
- Strong precision measurement reputation
- Extensive reference designs (Circuits from the Lab)

### Primary Competitors

#### 1. Texas Instruments (TI)
- **Strengths**: Broad portfolio, competitive pricing, strong brand
- **Weaknesses**: Less focus on ultra-high precision
- **Overlap**: Data converters, amplifiers, power management

#### 2. Maxim Integrated (now part of ADI)
- **Strengths**: Power management, interface products
- **Note**: Acquired by ADI, portfolio being integrated

#### 3. Linear Technology (now part of ADI)
- **Strengths**: High-performance power, precision analog
- **Note**: Acquired by ADI, portfolio being integrated

### Our Competitive Advantages as Distributor
1. **Authorized status** - Direct ADI partnership
2. **Local FAE support** - Technical assistance in Chinese
3. **Access to full portfolio** - Including Maxim/Linear products
4. **Fast delivery** - Local inventory
5. **Reference designs** - Circuits from the Lab access
