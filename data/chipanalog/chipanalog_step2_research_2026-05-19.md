## 1. Brand Overview

### Company Profile
- **Full Name:** 川土微电子 (Chipanalog Microelectronics)
- **Founded:** 2016
- **Headquarters:** Shanghai, China
- **Position:** China's leading isolation technology company
- **Focus:** High-performance isolation products for industrial, automotive, and energy applications

### Core Products
- **Digital Isolators:** Multi-channel digital signal isolation
- **Isolated Gate Drivers:** IGBT/MOSFET gate drivers with isolation
- **Isolated Interfaces:** CAN, RS-485, I2C isolated transceivers
- **Isolated ADCs:** Precision isolated analog-to-digital converters
- **Isolated Power:** Integrated isolated DC-DC converters

### Key Series
- **CA-IS37xx:** Digital isolators with integrated DC-DC
- **CA-IS3211:** Single-channel isolated gate driver
- **CA-IS3221:** Dual-channel isolated gate driver
- **CA-IS3417:** Isolated RS-485 transceiver
- **CA-IS3430:** Isolated CAN FD transceiver
- **CA-IS1300:** Isolated precision ADC

### Certifications
- AEC-Q100 Automotive Grade
- UL 1577 Safety Certification
- VDE 0884-11 Isolation Certification
- ISO 9001 Quality Management
- CQC Certification (China)
