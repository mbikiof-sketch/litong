## 1. Brand Overview

### Company Profile
- **Full Name:** 江苏长电科技股份有限公司 (Jiangsu Changdian Technology Co., Ltd.)
- **Founded:** 1972 (Over 50 years of expertise)
- **Headquarters:** Wuxi, Jiangsu, China
- **Position:** China's leading power semiconductor discrete devices manufacturer
- **Stock Code:** 600584.SH (Shanghai Stock Exchange)

### Core Products
- **Diodes:** Standard rectifier, fast recovery, Schottky barrier
- **Rectifiers:** Bridge rectifiers, single-phase, three-phase modules
- **MOSFETs:** N-channel, P-channel, logic level, power MOSFETs
- **Transistors:** Bipolar junction transistors (BJTs)
- **Protection Devices:** TVS, Zener diodes

### Key Series
- **1N400x Series:** Standard rectifier diodes (1A, 50-1000V)
- **1N540x Series:** High current rectifier diodes (3A, 50-1000V)
- **FR Series:** Fast recovery diodes (500ns trr)
- **MBR Series:** Schottky barrier diodes (low VF)
- **GBJ/KBPC Series:** Bridge rectifiers (6A-50A)
- **IRF Series:** Power MOSFETs (TO-220/TO-247)

### Certifications
- AEC-Q101 Automotive Grade
- ISO 9001 Quality Management
- IATF 16949 Automotive Quality
- RoHS/REACH Compliant
