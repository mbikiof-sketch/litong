## 1. Brand Overview

### Company Profile
- **Full Name:** Nippon Chemi-Con Corporation (日本贵弥功株式会社)
- **Founded:** 1931 (Over 90 years of expertise)
- **Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Employees:** 5000+
- **Revenue:** $800M+
- **Position:** Japan's largest aluminum electrolytic capacitor manufacturer

### Core Products
- Aluminum Electrolytic Capacitors (铝电解电容器)
- Radial Lead Capacitors (径向引线电容器)
- Snap-in Capacitors (卡入式电容器)
- Screw Terminal Capacitors (螺丝端子电容器)
- Solid Polymer Capacitors (固态聚合物电容器)
- Automotive-grade Capacitors (AEC-Q200 qualified)

### Key Series
- **KMQ Series:** Premium radial lead, highest reliability
- **KZE Series:** General-purpose, excellent value
- **KY Series:** High-temperature (130°C)
- **LXS Series:** Snap-in for high-power applications
- **ALS Series:** Screw terminal for very high power
- **AKM Series:** AEC-Q200 qualified automotive
